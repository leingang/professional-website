Today I ran one of my favorite local stretches, through Black Brook Park.  It's
woodsy and secluded, but the  trail is paved.  To get to the park I have to run
along a golf course, and once I emerge  I end up on the “Nomahegan Mile” (that
may only be official to Strava).  It's a berm through the park that's straight
and fast.

After that it's Echo Lake Park, then in to Westfield and back home along North Ave.
The last part is pretty boring.  If I need to add miles to the route I take turns
that avoid the thoroughfares.

No wildlife spotted other than rabbits.  Usually in the park I see a deer or two.

The speed was quicker than comfortable.  This is one of the `Hansons' <http://www.hansons-running.com/>`__ “long” runs,
which refer not just to distance but pacing.  It's about 30 seconds per mile off
goal pace.  I was trying to do 7:42 min miles.  The first miles were quite a bit slower
as I got warmed up, but I finished strong and did 7:11 on the last mile to bring
the average down to 7:43.
