I am a normally happy `Citi Bike`_ user.  I take it back and forth from Port Authority Bus Terminal to NYU.  (One of my dreams came true a couple of years ago when they `profiled me`_.) One thing I don't like is not being able to find a bike when I need one.

In the weekday mornings the stations around PABT are pretty empty.  Last summer I had to routinely walk to Penn Station to get a bike.  That takes a lot of extra time, and not a small amount of energy.  And some days even Penn Station had no bikes nearby.  Once I walked all the way to 26th street before I found one—pretty much halfway to work.

So I was really excited to discover that Citi Bike had placed a valet station at 42nd & Dyer.  They have probably hundreds of bikes parked in a nearby garage, and they have two workers just bringing them out and docking them.  The valets at this station are particularly friendly, too, wishing everyone a good day as they head off to work.

To head to the station from Port Authority I have to walk away from my eventual direction.  But it's only a block west of the terminal, on a two-way street.  Access to 9th Avenue (one-way, downtown, with protected bike lane) is pretty easy, and then I take get 30th street East to Broadway, avoiding Herald Square.

Anyway, I came to work later than usual today and discovered no bikes even at 42nd & Dyer.  They say the service runs until 9:45am but they had run out bikes, even there.

This is what the map looked like at that point:

No bikes at the four closest stations.  There were two at 38th & Broadway.  No guarantees they would be there when I got there.  After that it was 34th and 7th Ave.

I understand Citi Bike is a victim of their own success.  The valet stations are a pretty good solution to high demand at commuting points.  And its serves me right for being so late to work.

I took the subway.
