Once again, the summer has vanished.  Fall classes begin today.  Like the students,
I begin the year with excitement, anxiety, and hope.

It's the beginning of my 10th year at NYU.  That means in a year I'll be invited
to a "longer service recognition" reception.  

The sidewalks are bustling with eager students, gnawing bagels and walking fast.
I heard some of them helping others out with directions.  And this is only at 9:15.
By the middle of the day the crowds will be even bigger.

I'm teaching an honors section of Calculus III and a section of Discrete Mathematics.
I haven't taught discrete in a long time, and I've never taught Calculus III out of
the honors textbook (Marsden & Tromba).  I'm hoping my students will be stimulated, 
challenged, and engaged.

I've got a fresh set of `Human Bingo`_ cards created.  One of my summer projects was 
beefing up an old python script to create them (with custom fields from a data file)
into a bona fide `PyPI project`_.

.. _`Human Bingo`: http://www.wikihow.com/Play-Human-Bingo
.. _`PyPI project`: https://pypi.python.org/pypi/humanbingo

And, once again, I have to download rosters from various websites (our SIS and LMS sites)
and upload them to other sites.  Having polished command-line tools makes this job so
much easier.  Especially since I included documentation or at least :code:`--help`.
