Last night I registered for the `Staten Island Half Marathon`_ on October 8.
Five years ago that race was my first half marathon, and I'm looking forward
to giving it another shot.  Once again I'll shoot for a sub-90 minute race.

As a coincidence, this morning lines up perfectly with the first day of my
standard 18-week training plan.  Since the Boston Marathon seven weeks ago,
I have built my base back up to 30-40 miles per week.  But starting Monday,
it's time to get serious about speed once again.

Today was an easy 6.2 miles.  The map looks funny because for the first mile
I had the GPS off on my watch—oops.  This often happens on days after 
treadmill runs.  

For the second time this week, I saw a deer on my run, and I regretted not
bringing my phone to snap a picture.  The road I was on cuts through a golf
course, and most of the course is lined with a fence.  The deer emerged
through the fence on one side of the road, crossed the road, but couldn't 
find the break in the fence on the other side.  As I reached her, she 
scampered away from me, but was hemmed in by the path in front of me and the
fence.  After about 100 yards of this, I crossed to the other side of the 
road, and she was able to backtrack behind me.
