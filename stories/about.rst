I earned my doctorate in mathematics from Harvard in 2000.
Since then I have been dedicated to mathematics, teaching, and technology in education.
I have held positions at `Rutgers University`_ and `Harvard University`_.
Since 2008 I have been on the clinical faculty at `New York University`_.

.. _`Rutgers University` : http://www.rutgers.edu/
.. _`Harvard University` : http://www.harvard.edu/
.. _`New York University` : http://www.nyu.edu/

I have also always enjoyed computing since I first programmed ASCII art on the
Apple II. In 1995 I put up my first website, not much more than a few pages of
links and photos. Over the years I have managed websites in WordPress and
Drupal. Now I'm exploring static site generators (currently Nikola_). I've
contributed to open-sources projects such as PHPSlash_ and MathBookXML_. My
current favorite language is python, but I've also worked in perl and PHP.

.. _Nikola: https://getnikola.com/
.. _PHPSlash: https://sourceforge.net/projects/phpslash/
.. _MathBookXML: https://mathbook.pugetsound.edu/

I've given talks at mathematics meetings about many diverse topics, including
inquiry-based learning, training graduate student teaching assistants,  teaching geometry to middle and
high school teachers, Web 2.0 and social media in math education, and how to
make good lecture slides.

I live in New Jersey with my wife and two children.  I like to cook, to run, and
to tinker around with light home-improvement projects.
